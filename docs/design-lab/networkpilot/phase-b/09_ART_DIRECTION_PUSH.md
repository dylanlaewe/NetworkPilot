# Phase B.2 Art Direction Push

Phase B.2 keeps the approved Network Command interaction model and changes only the isolated prototype’s art direction. No production behavior is represented or modified.

## Visual signature

Network Command now uses one continuous work trace:

- the selected queue person receives a precise green edge and a warm correspondence surface;
- the trace enters the message plane at the recipient header;
- it continues into the lifecycle dock and transfer states;
- uncertain work changes the trace’s symbol and tone without changing the composition.

This is the product signature. It does not depend on the wordmark, avatars, cards, gradients, shadows, or decorative illustration.

## Composition changes

### Today

- The first task is materially larger than subordinate work.
- A short structural rule, active trace, and asymmetric count establish a prepared command surface.
- Selected work carries a stronger human identity block.
- Live State is a lower-contrast operational rail rather than an appended dashboard module.

### Drafts

- Desktop proportions are now message first, queue second, inspector third.
- The queue uses tone and internal rhythm instead of a separator after every field.
- The selected row changes surface and identity weight instead of receiving a spreadsheet-style fill.
- The message canvas is warm and bright; the inspector is cooler, quieter, and narrower.

### Message plane

- Recipient identity and subject are the two dominant landmarks.
- The attachment selector is a compact contextual strip.
- Body measure and paragraph rhythm resemble considered correspondence rather than a form.
- Approved states remain visibly sealed through state trace, locked type, and immutable-version language.

### Candidates

- Five inventory columns collapse into three ideas: person/current work, why now, and context.
- Role and company stay attached to the person.
- Lane and availability share a quiet context region.
- The inspector begins with the human and the reason to consider them, not record metadata.

### Consequential surfaces

- The action dock uses a stable operating footprint across Approve, Create Gmail Draft, Send Email, and Resolve Send Status.
- Send confirmation uses a compact correspondence hierarchy and one explicit consequence sentence.
- The command palette adds NetworkPilot context, a lifecycle trace, and a current-workspace marker without imitating a general-purpose launcher.

## Border audit

Continuous pane borders, row boxes, field outlines, and repeated section rules were removed where spacing, tone, alignment, or selection already expressed structure. Remaining rules identify a real boundary: workspace chrome, immutable content, a contextual region, or a consequential transition.

## State grammar

- Needs review: open-circle trace and active review language.
- Approved: check trace and sealed snapshot treatment.
- Gmail created: square transfer trace and Send Email action.
- Uncertain: question trace, amber local tone, and reconciliation language.
- Sent: transfer feedback and removal from the active queue.
- Suppressed: destructive symbol plus explicit permanent-exclusion language.
- Unavailable: quiet text plus a non-color status symbol.

## Internal critique

- The result no longer reads primarily as “Linear with green”; correspondence hierarchy and the work trace provide a more specific visual identity.
- Drafts no longer reads as a generic admin workspace because the message is visibly dominant and secondary evidence recedes.
- People read before provider or operational metadata.
- Today is authored through hierarchy and rhythm without becoming a dashboard or hero page.
- Candidates remains dense but now frames opportunity rather than inventory fields.
- The visual hierarchy is legible within two seconds at wide, medium, and mobile widths.
- The restrained tone is intended to remain credible in 2028; it avoids current component-library fashions.

## Remaining weaknesses

- Today still relies on deliberate open space; real production work volume must confirm that the balance does not feel under-filled.
- At 1024 px the inspector is intentionally terse and would need production-grade progressive disclosure.
- Compact candidate density will always feel more operational than comfortable density.
- The art direction’s distinctiveness is quiet; careless production spacing would weaken it quickly.
