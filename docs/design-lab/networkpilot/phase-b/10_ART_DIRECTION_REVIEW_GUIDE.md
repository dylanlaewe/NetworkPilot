# Phase B.2 Review Guide

All captures contain deterministic fictional prototype data. Contact sheets:

## Desktop

1. **Today** — Check whether the work hierarchy is immediate and the open space feels deliberate.
2. **Drafts 5** — Check whether a short queue still feels integrated with the dominant correspondence plane.
3. **Drafts 15** — Check the normal queue rhythm, selected-person trace, message measure, and quiet inspector.
4. **Drafts 30** — Check whether long-queue density preserves human identity.
5. **Approved** — Check whether the snapshot feels sealed without paper or badge metaphors.
6. **Uncertain** — Check whether uncertainty is distinct from failure, success, and ordinary review.
7. **Candidates** — Check whether rows read as people/current work and relevance rather than inventory.
8. **Command palette** — Check whether the dark surface feels native to NetworkPilot rather than a stock launcher.
9. **Send confirmation** — Check recipient, subject, consequence, and action hierarchy.
10. **Send success** — Check whether transfer feedback preserves queue continuity.

## Medium widths

11. **Drafts 1024** — Check that the inspector compresses before the correspondence measure does.
12. **Drafts 1280** — Check the transition between terse and full secondary context.

## Mobile

13. **Today** — Check primary-task dominance, selected-person context, and integrated Live State.
14. **Draft queue** — Check touch density, selected-person precision, and quiet state labels.
15. **Draft review** — Check recipient/subject hierarchy, body rhythm, and bottom-dock tactility.
16. **Candidates** — Check name, role, company, and reduced right-edge metadata.
17. **Filter sheet** — Check contextual continuity and touch spacing.

## Open review questions

- Is the work trace recognizable without becoming a logo device?
- Is the message unmistakably the emotional center?
- Does Today’s open lower field feel intentional rather than unfinished?
- Do Candidates rows feel human enough at repeated daily-use density?
- Is the inspector sufficiently quiet at 1280 and sufficiently useful at 1024?
- Does the send confirmation feel calm and consequential?
- Does the command palette belong to this product?
- Is the light neutral palette premium rather than clinical?
- Is any remaining surface visibly template-generated?
