## Desktop

### 01 — Today

- **Shows:** Work-first command composition with selected-person preview and integrated Live State.
- **Demonstrates:** Hierarchy through type, rules, and space rather than cards or metrics.
- **Review critically:** Whether the primary task owns the first viewport and whether unused space feels deliberate.

### 02 — Drafts, 5 items

- **Shows:** The joined queue, message, inspector, and action dock with a short queue.
- **Demonstrates:** One continuous working surface with correspondence as the hero.
- **Review critically:** Empty-queue balance, shared edges, selected-row continuity, and inspector restraint.

### 03 — Drafts, 15 items

- **Shows:** Normal working density across lifecycle groups.
- **Demonstrates:** Person-first scanning and a stable review composition at daily scale.
- **Review critically:** Queue rhythm, message measure, identity hierarchy, and dock balance.

### 04 — Drafts, 30 items

- **Shows:** High queue density without changing the working architecture.
- **Demonstrates:** Long-list continuity and quiet operational metadata.
- **Review critically:** Whether the queue still feels human and whether the message remains dominant.

### 05 — Approved state

- **Shows:** A sealed approved snapshot and Create Gmail Draft as the next action.
- **Demonstrates:** Read-only correspondence and lifecycle state without skeuomorphism.
- **Review critically:** Whether sealing is unmistakable without making the message feel inert.

### 06 — Uncertain state

- **Shows:** A retained draft with unresolved external send status.
- **Demonstrates:** Caution through label, glyph, accent, and action language rather than warning decoration.
- **Review critically:** Whether uncertainty is distinct from both error and success.

### 07 — Candidates, comfortable density

- **Shows:** Person, current work, lane, concise relevance cue, quiet status, and selected inspector.
- **Demonstrates:** Opportunity browsing without profile cards or inventory-table emphasis.
- **Review critically:** Identity strength, human scan order, and inspector relationship.

### 08 — Command palette

- **Shows:** Navigation and contextual commands with current-workspace and unavailable-command treatment.
- **Demonstrates:** A native NetworkPilot command layer rather than a generic launcher.
- **Review critically:** Group rhythm, selected state, shortcut utility, and disabled explanations.

### 09 — Send confirmation

- **Shows:** Recipient, company, subject, attachment, approved version, and external consequence.
- **Demonstrates:** A compact consequential state without alarm-modal styling.
- **Review critically:** Whether the decision is sufficiently deliberate and trustworthy.

### 15 — Drafts at 1024 px

- **Shows:** Condensed queue and inspector around a protected message plane.
- **Demonstrates:** Progressive density instead of simply crushing every column.
- **Review critically:** Inspector terseness, subject truncation, and correspondence reading width.

### 16 — Drafts at 1280 px

- **Shows:** The same workspace at an intermediate desktop width.
- **Demonstrates:** Pane proportions that expand the message before secondary evidence.
- **Review critically:** The transition from condensed to full inspector content.

## Mobile

### 10 — Today

- **Shows:** Ordered work, selected-person context, integrated Live State, and bottom navigation.
- **Demonstrates:** A work-first mobile composition rather than stacked desktop cards.
- **Review critically:** Primary-task clarity and where Live State enters the scroll.

### 11 — Draft queue

- **Shows:** Counts, grouped lifecycle rows, identity hierarchy, selection, and Add drafts.
- **Demonstrates:** Queue-first entry with dense but touch-usable rows.
- **Review critically:** Long-role truncation, state legibility, and selection confidence.

### 12 — Draft review

- **Shows:** Full-screen recipient, custom attachment selector, subject, body, and sticky action dock.
- **Demonstrates:** Independent mobile correspondence review.
- **Review critically:** Reading rhythm, identity header, subject scale, and dock tactility.

### 13 — Candidates

- **Shows:** Person-first rows with role, company, lane, and quiet availability.
- **Demonstrates:** Mobile opportunity browsing without importing desktop metadata.
- **Review critically:** Whether identity increased without making rows taller or noisier.

### 14 — Filter sheet

- **Shows:** Track, role-lane, and region filtering in a contextual bottom sheet.
- **Demonstrates:** Structured filtering without permanent control clutter.
- **Review critically:** Touch spacing, dismissibility, and retained context.

## Open visual questions

- Is the joined workspace distinct enough to avoid reading as “Linear with green”?
- Does the light palette feel premium or too clinical?
- Is person identity strong enough during repeated daily use?
- Does Today feel authored rather than merely organized?
- Does Candidates feel like opportunity rather than inventory?
- Is the compact candidate density too operational?
- Is the inspector too terse at 1024 px?
- Does the custom resume selector feel intentional enough without becoming a form control showcase?
- Does the action dock communicate consequence without encouraging throughput?
- Does the command palette feel integral to the product?
- Does mobile feel independently composed?
- Is any part of the system still visibly template-like?
